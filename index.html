About VSIMS

Vidyashekhar Institutions of Management Studies is committed to offering top-tier business education tailored to foster personal success. Our fundamental values emphasize developing students into independent learners, cooperative team members, discerning thinkers, and creators of high-quality work who also act as ethical participants in society — with the utmost standards of academic rigor, mutual respect and societal awareness. The main goal at our institution is to propel career advancement.

Programs

Two postgraduate programs, offered in hybrid mode.

Postgraduate

MBA

Master of Business Administration — a management program designed to prepare students for the dynamic world of business, with an industry-oriented curriculum.

Postgraduate

MCA

Master of Computer Applications — a computing program under Bangalore University, building strong foundations in software, systems and modern application development.
